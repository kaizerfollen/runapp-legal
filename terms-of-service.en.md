## 1. What Runcell is

Runcell is a fitness app with gameplay elements:

- You record walks, runs and bike rides
- You capture territory on an H3-hexagon map
- You earn coins and experience points

- You join clans, chat, and post in the clan feed

**Runcell is not a medical device.** We don't provide medical or training
advice. Exercise mindfully based on your physical condition and consult a
doctor if needed.
